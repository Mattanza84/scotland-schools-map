Aberdeen and North East

Dynamic cities and coastal towns with strong communities.

View schools on map →

Aberdeen and the North East is a region of contrasts — from the energy and ambition of Aberdeen City to the quiet fishing villages of Moray and the rolling farmland of Aberdeenshire. The region covers three local authorities, each offering a different pace of life while sharing a strong sense of community and a proud local identity.

Explore school performance, compare local house prices and crime data, and discover the communities that best match your family's needs.

WHY FAMILIES CHOOSE Aberdeen and the North East

From Aberdeen's vibrant harbour city to the Aberdeenshire countryside and the dramatic Moray coast, the North East of Scotland offers families an exceptional quality of life with strong schools and a resilient local economy.

Strong Schools

A well-regarded collection of primary and secondary schools across Aberdeen City, Aberdeenshire and Moray, with many consistently achieving strong results.

Great Places to Live

From Aberdeen's granite city centre to Aberdeenshire market towns and Moray's coastal villages, there's a community to suit every family.

Good Connections

Rail links to Edinburgh and Glasgow, Aberdeen International Airport and well-maintained roads connect the North East to the rest of Scotland and beyond.

Coast and Countryside

Miles of dramatic coastline, the Cairngorms National Park and the Castle Trail make the North East a brilliant base for outdoor families.

Resilient Economy

Traditionally strong in energy and agriculture, Aberdeen's economy is increasingly diversified across life sciences, tech and the creative industries.

Proud Communities

A strong sense of local identity, warm communities and a rich cultural heritage make Aberdeen and the North East a great place to put down roots.
